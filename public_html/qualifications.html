Thesis project

Final grade: A-

For my undergraduate thesis, I built an AI system using genetic programming that learned how to play a board game called Agon. The program, written in Lua, created, combined, and evolved board-evaluation functions. The evolved strategies beat a random opponent within four generations and continued to improve.
